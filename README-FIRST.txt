KK FLORIDA COMPLETE WEBSITE

Upload all files and folders to the top level of your GitHub repository KarenK55/kk-florida-website. Commit the changes. Cloudflare Pages will redeploy automatically.

Edit data/site.json to change text, links, products, collections, and guide cards.

The starter photos are placeholders. Upload your own photos to assets/images and replace image URLs in data/site.json with paths such as assets/images/cardinal.jpg.

After deployment, open your Cloudflare Pages project, choose Custom domains, and connect the domain you purchase.
