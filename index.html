About KK Florida

Inspired by the color and character of Florida.

KK Florida celebrates Florida wildlife and the beauty of nature through original photography and artwork. Discover birds, squirrels, butterflies, bees, dragonflies, orchids, tropical flowers, colorful sunsets and Everglades landscapes. The photographs are created in South Florida and inspired by the natural beauty of Broward County and beyond.
